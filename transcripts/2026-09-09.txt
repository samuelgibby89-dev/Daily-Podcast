MarketWatch's word was "underwhelmed," and that tells you something about how much firepower a buyback program actually has against a real inflation scare.

This isn't a one-country story — it's global energy anxiety plus a trade war that keeps compounding. Canada's retaliatory tariffs, worth twenty-seven point six billion Canadian dollars, took effect yesterday, and the U.S. just announced fresh import bans on Canadian motorbikes, alcohol, and dairy set for later this month. That rift is not cooling off.
