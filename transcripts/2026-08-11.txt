Good morning. The AI trade had a strong night, and CoreWeave was the headline. Not every AI name got the same reception, though — Lumentum posted blowout numbers and an upbeat forecast and still traded flat, a reminder that "beat and raise" isn't automatically rewarded anymore when expectations are this stretched. Meanwhile Nvidia's Jensen Huang is pitching GPUs as collateral for a five-hundred-billion-dollar financing plan, and the open question is how fast that collateral actually depreciates, especially with Chinese competition in the mix.

Outside tech, oil pushed back above eighty-three dollars a barrel. Iran said the Strait of Hormuz stays closed until its conditions are met, and that came right after Trump demanded reparations from Tehran. That's a real geopolitical premium building in crude, not just a supply story — U.S. stockpiles actually rose nine point one million barrels last week per the API data, so this is about risk, not scarcity.
