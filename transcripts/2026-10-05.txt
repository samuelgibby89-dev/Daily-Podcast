Without hard index numbers from the data feed this morning, here's what we do know: the broader tape held up well enough that the bond rout didn't knock it off its record pace. That's the headline for session positioning today.

On sectors, the story is metals and memory. Cleveland-Cliffs rose 8% as part of a broader metals rebound. Western Digital and Seagate bounced back as analysts pushed back on fears that Toshiba could meaningfully dent their market share anytime soon. Fixed income is really the whole plot right now. We don't have the exact basis-point moves this morning, but the direction of travel — higher yields, flatter enthusiasm for long duration — is consistent with a market still working out whether growth or inflation wins the next few quarters.
