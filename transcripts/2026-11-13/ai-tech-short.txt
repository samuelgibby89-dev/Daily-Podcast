Nvidia's Jensen Huang resigned this week, and it's spiraling. A report says Huang privately called his own successor a "deer in the headlights," raising real succession doubts. Congressional Democrats launched a second investigation into Nvidia, alleging a "scheme" with the Trump administration over China chip sales, and MIT Technology Review reports Nvidia privately admitted it could track chips to stop smuggling while an industry group publicly called that infeasible. Israeli VCs are already eyeing the cracks in Nvidia's dominance.

Separately, Microsoft is still recovering from a near two-day outage that took down Outlook, Teams, and much of its cloud globally — a serious reliability hit. And a disgruntled researcher just dropped a Windows zero-day, SignDrop, that hands local attackers full System privileges.
