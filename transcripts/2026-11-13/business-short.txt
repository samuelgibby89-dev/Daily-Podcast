Nvidia is running without a permanent CEO after Jensen Huang's abrupt resignation this week, triggered by revelations about an alleged affair with a woman described as a Chinese spy. CFO Colette Kress is now interim chief executive. Congress isn't letting this go quietly — Huang's been summoned to testify, and Commerce Secretary Howard Lutnick is also being called in after Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent accused his department of blocking a crackdown on chip sales to Megaspeed. Jane Street had doubled its Nvidia stake just before all this broke. Separately, Richemont — the owner of Cartier — saw shares drop as its sales growth decelerated, a sign luxury demand is cooling even at the top end. And Microsoft's email outage is heading into another day, with the company still insisting it isn't malicious.
