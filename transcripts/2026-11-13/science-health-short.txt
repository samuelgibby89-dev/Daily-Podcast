A new mouse study found that viral infections can leave lasting damage to the immune system, weakening the body's defenses long after symptoms clear. Prior vaccination completely prevented this in the mice studied. It's an animal study, so it's not proof the same happens in people, but it points to a mechanism where vaccines protect more than just against the one illness. Separately, Oregon logged its first H5N1 case in a dairy herd, and a farmworkers' union says human bird flu cases are being undercounted in Washington state. And watch the hype around microdosing Ozempic as an anti-aging treatment — there's essentially no clinical evidence behind that claim yet.
