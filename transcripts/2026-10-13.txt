That's not a forecast to ignore; it's the kind of warning that tends to get quoted back later.

Paul Tudor Jones added his own note of caution, saying 2027 could be a genuinely hard year for stocks as the buyback and M&A tailwinds that have powered this bull run start to fade. Worth sitting with, even if it's a year out.

On the calendar: tomorrow's CPI is the main event, and Fed speakers will likely be parsing it in real time. If you only read one thing today, make it the IMF's piece on disorderly bond yields — it's a sober look at a risk that's been easy to ignore while equities have been calm.

One more small story worth knowing: Anthropic is set to disclose new revenue numbers to investors this week, which should sharpen the debate over AI lab valuations heading into year-end.
