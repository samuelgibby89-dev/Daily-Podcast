Nvidia's Jensen Huang has resigned as chief executive, tangled up in reports about a relationship with a woman tied to stakes in Huawei-linked firms — though the Justice Department says it hasn't concluded she was a spy. Nvidia stock fell five percent, and Congress is now pushing harder on China chip curbs. Despite that, the S&P 500 closed down just four tenths of a percent, the Nasdaq off four tenths — small caps took the real hit, with the Russell 2000 down one point seven percent. Technology actually led all sectors, up three point two percent, as money rotated into names like Cerebras and Samsung. The bigger move was in bonds: the ten-year yield jumped twelve basis points to five ten, its sharpest surge since 2022, as the market prices in a more hawkish Fed.
