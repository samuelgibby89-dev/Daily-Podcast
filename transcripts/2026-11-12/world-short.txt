The Ukraine peace process just widened. After trilateral talks in Abu Dhabi produced a prisoner exchange deal, US envoys Steve Witkoff and Jared Kushner briefed Vladimir Putin in Moscow, then flew to Dublin to meet Volodymyr Zelensky. Kushner says Russia and Ukraine have agreed the next round of talks will include European nations — a real shift, though EU governments are still fighting over who gets the seat. Fighting continues regardless: a Ukrainian drone hit a Russian warship in the Gulf of Finland, apparently the first such strike in open Baltic waters.

Separately, Nvidia's Jensen Huang resigned as chief executive after a second affair surfaced, tangled up with US concerns that one relationship involved alleged Chinese intelligence recruitment — a blow to the company at the center of the AI chip race with China.
