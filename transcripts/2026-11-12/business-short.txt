Jensen Huang has resigned as Nvidia's CEO after revelations of two extramarital affairs, one reportedly with a suspected Chinese spy. Finance chief Colette Kress is interim CEO while the board, led by new chairman Harvey Jones, searches for a permanent replacement, with names like Lisa Su and Tim Cook floated. Huang's personal fortune has already dropped twelve percent this week. Lawmakers want him to testify, and Commerce Secretary Howard Lutnick is facing questions over Nvidia's China chip sales. The bigger worry is for the data center operators whose financing leaned on an implicit Nvidia guarantee that now looks shakier.

Elsewhere, the UK economy grew zero point seven percent last quarter, its fastest pace in two years, while Metro Bank put itself up for sale and Poundland bought ten more days to find a rescue buyer.
