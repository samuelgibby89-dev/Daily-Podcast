Nvidia's chief executive Jensen Huang resigned yesterday following reports of affairs, including one with an alleged Chinese spy — and Reuters now says there was a third relationship with another Nvidia employee. The company's CFO is running things on an interim basis while Washington piles on: Democrats have opened a second congressional investigation, this time into Huang's dealings with Trump and Nvidia's China sales. Inside Nvidia, employees are pushing the board to bring him back. Separately, Google rolled out Gemini four Hydrogen, which it says handles complicated, multi-part questions better, and it's now powering upgrades to Google's AI Mode in Search. Worth knowing either way: the chipmaker running point on the US-China AI race currently has no permanent chief executive.
