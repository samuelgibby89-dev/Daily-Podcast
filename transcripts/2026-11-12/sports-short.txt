Baseball's MVP awards both came in unanimous last night. Pete Crow-Armstrong won National League MVP over Shohei Ohtani after a huge turnaround season for the Cubs. Yordan Alvarez took American League MVP for the Astros, bouncing back from an injury-hit 2025 to nearly complete a Triple Crown. In football, Harry Kane scored the winner from the spot as England beat Croatia two-one at Wembley to reach the Nations League quarter-finals, and the goal made him England's most-capped player in history. Manchester United have also agreed a deal with Mauricio Pochettino to become their next head coach. Tonight brings Thursday Night Football, Commanders hosting the Giants.
