The American Cancer Society reversed sixteen years of guidance, now urging routine PSA testing for men fifty to sixty-nine — a guideline shift based on re-read evidence, not a new trial. Separately, a CRISPR therapy that edits genes directly inside the body, without removing a patient's cells first, looks set for a first FDA approval — a real logistical leap from earlier gene-editing treatments. Worth a skeptical note: microdosed Ozempic is being marketed online as an anti-aging drug, but there's no solid human evidence behind that specific use. The real GLP-one benefits are proven at prescribed doses for diabetes and obesity, not at longevity microdoses.
